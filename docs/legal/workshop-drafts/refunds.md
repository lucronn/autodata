# AutoData Workshop Refund Policy

**Proposed policy for owner approval — November 6, 2026. Not effective or
published. The 14-day guarantee and resolution period below are proposed
customer commitments, not an existing approved refund practice.**

AutoData, LLC, based in Georgia, USA, sells online access to one selected vehicle
configuration and region for a one-time **USD $20 total**. There is no
subscription to cancel.

## Our proposed 14-day guarantee

Request a full refund within 14 calendar days after payment for any reason,
including selecting the wrong configuration or changing your mind. Accessing
or reading the dataset does not remove this guarantee. We will refund the full
amount paid for that purchase, including any tax included in the total.

## Duplicate charges and delivery problems

Contact us whenever you identify a duplicate charge, cannot access your paid
dataset, or believe it materially differs from the description shown when you
bought it. We will investigate and correct confirmed duplicate charges. For an
access or description problem, we will work with you to restore access or
provide the described content. If we cannot resolve the problem within seven
calendar days after you report it, we will offer a full refund unless you agree
to give us more time. These remedies also apply when a problem is reported
after the 14-day guarantee; mandatory legal rights may provide other remedies.

If we permanently stop providing purchased access, including because necessary
source rights are withdrawn, contact us for an appropriate remedy. We will
consider the affected purchase and applicable law. This does not promise a
fixed lifetime for the service or waive mandatory consumer rights.

## How to request help or a refund

Email [support@cars.tk](mailto:support@cars.tk). Include your order reference if
available, a reply email address, the vehicle configuration and region, and a
short explanation. An order reference is helpful but is not required to ask for
help. Do not send full card details, passwords, or authentication codes. We may
ask for limited information to verify that you own the purchase.

If an order is still processing, contact support before paying again. A retry
or content preparation delay does not require another purchase. Signing out
alone does not request a refund.

## Payment and access after a refund

We issue approved refunds to the original payment method where supported. If
that method is unavailable, we will work with you and the payment provider on
an available return method. Your payment provider or financial institution
controls when the funds appear; we will confirm when we have issued the refund.
A full refund or reversed payment removes access to the refunded dataset.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights or remedies. For
questions or complaints, use [Contact](contact.md). Read the [Terms](terms.md)
and [Privacy Notice](privacy.md) before purchasing.

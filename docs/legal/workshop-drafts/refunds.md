# AutoData Workshop Refund Policy

**Draft for owner review — November 6, 2026. Proposed process; not effective or
published. The discretionary refund terms remain an owner decision.**

AutoData, LLC sells online access to one selected vehicle configuration and
region for a one-time USD $20 total. There is no subscription to cancel.

## Getting help

If you were charged twice, paid but cannot access the purchased dataset, or
believe the delivered dataset materially differs from the description, email
[support@cars.tk](mailto:support@cars.tk). Include your order reference if
available, the email you can be reached at, the vehicle configuration and region,
and a short description of the problem. Do not include full card details,
passwords, or authentication codes.

**Proposed process:** We will investigate the payment and access records,
attempt to resolve delivery problems, and assess the appropriate remedy,
including a refund where required by applicable law. If you selected the wrong
configuration or changed your mind, contact us so we can review the request;
this draft does not establish a discretionary refund guarantee or refusal.

## Payment and access

If the order is still processing, contact support before paying again. A retry
or content preparation delay does not itself require another purchase.

An approved refund is returned through the original payment method where
supported. When the funds appear depends on the payment provider and your
financial institution; no fixed processing time is promised here. A full refund
or reversed payment can remove access to the refunded dataset. Signing out
alone does not request a refund.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights or remedies. Contact
[support@cars.tk](mailto:support@cars.tk) if you have a complaint or cannot
locate an order.
